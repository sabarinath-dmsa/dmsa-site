Divyaang Myithri Sports Academy

DMSA trains and supports para-athletes across Karnataka. Our camps, community programmes and tournaments, including the Karnataka Wheelchair Premier League (KWPL), all sit under this academy.

The DMSA app

The DMSA Android app lets supporters follow academy news, events and tournament results, log their contribution, and donate. Donors who need a tax receipt can request an 80G receipt in the app.
