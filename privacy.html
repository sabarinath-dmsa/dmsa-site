Privacy Policy

Divyaang Myithri Sports Academy (“DMSA”, “we”). Last updated: 19 October 2026.

This policy explains what information the DMSA Android app collects, why, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to it.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google name and email address only. We do not see your Google password. If you sign in with email and password, the password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we cannot read it.
  • Profile and roles: audience tags (for example volunteer or supporter) and, for DMSA staff, the admin roles they have been given.
  • Contribution records: volunteer hours, training, events and similar activities that DMSA staff log against your account.
  • Donation details: when you tell us about a donation, the amount, your UPI transaction reference if you enter one, your name and email. If you request an 80G tax receipt we also collect your PAN and postal address, which Indian tax rules require on the receipt.
  • Earlier tournament activity: information from past fan-league features, such as team selections and points.

We do not collect your location, contacts or device files. The app does not show advertising and does not use advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and show you the right content.
  • To record, confirm and acknowledge donations, and to prepare and send 80G receipts.
  • To run DMSA programmes, events and tournaments and to recognise volunteers and supporters.
  • To contact you about your donation, receipt or account.

Where it is stored and who can see it

  • Your data is stored with Google services we use to run the app: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Drive and Google Apps Script. These providers process data on our behalf.
  • Your private account record (email and similar) is visible only to you and to authorised DMSA staff. Other users can see only your display name and audience tag, for example on a leaderboard.
  • Donation records, including PAN and address, are visible only to you and to authorised DMSA staff. Copies of 80G receipts are kept in a private DMSA Google Drive folder.
  • We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers above, or where required by law, for example by income tax authorities.

How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account is active. Donation records and 80G receipts are kept for as long as tax and accounting rules require us to keep them.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct your details, or to delete your account and the data we are not legally required to keep, by emailing us at the address below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Some DMSA athletes are under 18. Accounts and donations for a child should be set up by a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us information without a guardian’s consent, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

We use access rules so that private records can be read only by their owner and by authorised staff, and we rely on the security of Google’s services. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in details private.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
